# Final 30-Second Competition Pitch

**Project**: Data Science Workforce Intelligence  
**Team**: The Processors  
**Event**: SAS CU Hackathon

## Elevator Pitch Script (Spoken Duration: ~30 Seconds)

> *"Good morning/afternoon. We are Team The Processors, and our project is **Data Science Workforce Intelligence**. We connect four complementary evidence layers: market demand, skills, junior-career outcomes, and senior professional success. We deliberately do not row-wise merge the datasets because there is no defensible common entity key. Our primary model uses Big Five personality dimensions to classify observed high/low success in the supplied SDS sample. A Random Forest achieved a cross-validated ROC-AUC of 0.9928 ± 0.0048, with grouped-ID and repeated-CV sensitivity checks. We frame these findings as observed statistical associations—not causal claims or automated hiring decisions. The system turns fragmented workforce evidence into traceable, grounded workforce strategy."*
